About

Operations discipline, carried from the plant floor into the fit-out site

I am an operations and project management professional with 2+ years across industrial construction and turnkey interior fit-out. Today I am Project Coordinator at FocusOn Interiors Pvt. Ltd., assisting the Project Director in delivering turnkey commercial interiors across Bengaluru, Chennai, Pune and Delhi.

My foundation was built at John Tech Engineers in Gujarat — scaffolding, hot and cold insulation, painting and plant maintenance for refineries, chemical plants and steel works. Running a job from quotation to final billing under a client's permit system teaches you that documentation is not paperwork; it is the project. I brought that habit with me: BOQ mapping workbooks, floor-wise execution plans, 10-sheet BOQ/MIS trackers, vendor comparisons and NCN closures.

Alongside project work I build the tools I wish existed — Next.js brand websites, Excel and Power BI cost systems, and AI-assisted project management prototypes for the fit-out industry. Everything I ship is published openly on GitHub.
