About Me

Operations discipline, from the plant floor to the fit-out site

I am an operations and project management professional with 2+ years across industrial construction and turnkey interior fit-out. Currently Project Coordinator at FocusOn Interiors Pvt. Ltd. (Gurugram HQ), assisting the Project Director in delivering turnkey commercial interiors across Bengaluru, Chennai, Pune and Delhi.

My foundation was built at John Tech Engineers (Sayan, Surat, Gujarat) — scaffolding, hot and cold insulation, painting and plant maintenance for refineries, chemical plants and steel works. Running a job from quotation to final billing under a client's permit system teaches you that documentation is not paperwork — it is the project.

Alongside project work, I build the tools I wish existed — Next.js brand websites, Excel and Power BI cost systems, and AI-assisted project management prototypes. Everything I ship is published openly on GitHub.
